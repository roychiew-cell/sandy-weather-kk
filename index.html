At a glance

Sandy's analysis for your place

Your location

Press the button and allow location once: the whole page then follows where you are. Within 15 km of Kota Kinabalu airport it shows the Kota Kinabalu dashboard; anywhere else (Singapore, Shanghai, Tokyo…) the top of the page, the At a glance tiles and the sky switch to your place, with a bar at the top to switch back. For each place Sandy picks the ten most reputable and reliable sources for it: the national forecast where an open feed exists (MetMalaysia, NEA, JMA), the nearest airport report, the ECMWF ensemble and the best-ranked models for the region. Nothing from Kota Kinabalu is used for another place.
What is sent whereYour coordinates, accuracy, acquisition time and location method are saved in this browser until you press Forget. Coordinates rounded to four decimal places go to Open-Meteo (forecast) and BigDataCloud (which country, state and district you are in). MetMalaysia receives forecast-location ids; the airport relay receives your position rounded to 0.1° (it uses only a 0.5° cell of it). NEA (Singapore) and JMA (Japan) receive no coordinates: the page downloads their forecasts and picks the nearest station or prefecture itself. Open-Meteo also receives them for the multi-model and ensemble requests, and the coordinates of the airport the page picked for the previous-day check. The page does not send your device position to GitHub. If the browser has already allowed location for this site, the page re-reads your position quietly when you come back to it. This card refreshes separately in each visible tab.
