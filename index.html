At a glance

Your location

Press the button and allow location once: the forecast for where you stand, from the nearest MetMalaysia records, the nearest airport and the models.
What is sent whereYour coordinates, accuracy, acquisition time and location method are saved in this browser until you press Forget. Coordinates rounded to four decimal places go to Open-Meteo (forecast) and BigDataCloud (which country, state and district you are in). MetMalaysia receives forecast-location ids; the airport relay receives an airport code. The page does not send your device position to GitHub. This card refreshes separately in each visible tab.

Next three parts of the day

7-day forecast

Morning / afternoon / night icons and temperatures: MetMalaysia's town record for each date, else its district record (tagged), else the model (tagged); district wording on hover when both exist. Bar and odds: ECMWF ensemble, chance of 10 mm+ at the airport grid point. Use the timeline below to run the week in the sky.

Next hours · rain chance and temperature

Bars = rain chance % for the hour starting at that position (Open-Meteo blend values are for the preceding hour, so each bar uses the value stored at the hour's end; a gap means no value), left axis. Line = temperature at each hour (right axis); the line breaks where a sample is missing. Band = TAF thunderstorm period. Times are MYT.

All sources · picked for Kota Kinabalu

Sources are chosen fresh for each location. For KK the order of trust is MetMalaysia and the airport's own readings first, then the models. "Live" sources are read by this page directly at the interval shown; "archive" sources come from the hourly GitHub job. "Reference" = an image or link for you to look at, not analysed by the page.
